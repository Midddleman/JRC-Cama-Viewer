Perennial river segment proportions

  • Scope: all study points or the selected basin.
  • Q: signed monthly mean discharge in 2019 (m³/s).
  • Bar height: perennial points / known-status points.
  • Empty groups: no known classifications, not 0%.
  • Thin lines: 95% Wilson intervals.
  • Direct comparison: bin raw discharge or count months with Q > QT.
  • Normalized: divide Q by the reference mean, then count months above the threshold (0–12).
  • Reference: study-wide mean for all points; basin mean for a selected basin.
  • Mean: arithmetic average of day-weighted annual means. Nonpositive references are not normalized.
  • Only complete 12-month records with every day valid participate.
  • Unknown JRC statuses are excluded from proportion denominators.
  • Each point has equal weight, not river-length weight.
  • 2019 flow versus JRC 1984–2021 status: association, not same-year validation.
